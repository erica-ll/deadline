# Peer playtest notes

These notes are anonymized and record the feedback artifact for the project
submission. Keep private feedback on CourseWorks as directed by the rubric.

## Feedback received on The Robner Case

See the classmate's detailed notes in [`peer-feedback.md`](peer-feedback.md).
The playtester reported a roughly 40-minute solo session on November 5. They
used browser translation and received hints about interview commands and
searching described objects, so some progress was not independent. The author
was not present to observe the session.

Main observations included:

- The case goal and opening premise were clear, but the player did not know
  how to make progress and accused George early to test the system.
- The action count and rejected-command call limit were confusing; the player
  thought rejected commands did not use up the available budget.
- Room names shown in the interface did not match the accepted capitalization.
  Other command targets and topics also had inconsistent case or exact-match
  rules.
- Generic refusal messages did not make prerequisites or the next step clear.
- The evidence list did not show who owned each medicine, and the short recent
  history made earlier answers difficult to find. The player repeated searches
  and interviews as a result.
- The Garden evidence chain, interrogation tension, and medicine comparison
  were effective deduction moments. The player found the ending and some
  character motivations unclear after opening the truth material.

These are the playtester's experiences and hypotheses, not verified diagnoses
or required design changes. The original feedback includes their detailed
examples and suggestions.
